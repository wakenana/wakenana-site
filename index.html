How it works

Three steps. Zero snoozing.

1

Set an alarm

Pick the time, the languages, and how many phrases it takes to shut Nana up.

2

Nana yells

A real voice says a slang phrase on repeat, even on silent. The words light up as they’re said.

3

Say it back

Right words first, then the accent. Get both and she finally goes quiet. Miss, and she tells you exactly what you got wrong.
