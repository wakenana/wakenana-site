Who can use Nana

You must be at least 17. Nana includes slang, crude humour, mild swearing and references to drinking.

Don't bet your flight on it

Nana works hard to wake you up, but phones can stop alarms: low battery, system updates, Focus or Do Not Disturb settings, force-quitting the app, or permissions you've turned off. Don't rely on Nana alone for anything that matters, like medication, flights or exams. Set a backup.

Your recordings

You own the phrases you record. By submitting one, you give Nana a worldwide, royalty-free licence to store it, play it to other users, let them download it, and use it to show off Nana. You can delete your phrases at any time in My phrases. Copies already downloaded to other people's phones disappear the next time they open Nana.

Only submit your own voice, and only phrases you have the right to share.

Crew rules

Keep it funny, not hateful. Don't submit slurs, hate speech, harassment, threats, sexual content, other people's personal information, someone else's voice without their permission, ads or spam. Translations must be honest.

There's zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. We review every phrase before it goes live and can refuse or remove any of them. A blocked phrase counts as a strike, and we may close the crew account of anyone who keeps breaking the rules.

Reports

If a phrase is offensive or wrong, report it from the phrase page. A phrase that gets three reports is taken down until we review it, and we look at every report within 24 hours. You can also block a crew member from any of their phrases: everything they recorded disappears from your phone and never rings. Unblock them in You → Privacy.
